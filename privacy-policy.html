LEGAL INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

Learn how MungPhai Trips & Tours collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use our website and travel services.

1. Privacy Policy

Information Collected

We collect the following personal information to provide our services:

  • Name
  • Mobile Number
  • Email Address
  • Travel dates
  • Pickup & Drop locations
  • Tour preferences
  • Booking details

How Information is Used

Your data is used strictly for operational and customer service purposes:

  • Process bookings
  • Contact customers
  • Improve services
  • Customer support
  • Payment processing

Who Information May Be Shared With

We do not sell your data. We only share it with trusted partners necessary to fulfill your trip:

  • Hotels
  • Local transport providers
  • Drivers
  • Payment gateways
  • Government authorities if legally required

Data Protection & Retention

We implement industry-standard security measures and secure storage to protect your information. We strictly prohibit the unauthorized selling of customer data. Booking records are retained only as necessary for business and legal purposes.

Customer Rights

Users have the right to request access to their information, correct inaccuracies, delete their information (where legally permitted), and contact our company regarding any privacy concerns.

2. Terms & Conditions

Booking Confirmation & Quotations

All quotations are subject to availability at the time of booking. A booking is only considered confirmed once the required advance payment is successfully processed and acknowledged by MungPhai Trips & Tours.

Payment Terms

Standard payment structures require an initial deposit upon booking, with the balance payable as per the mutually agreed terms before or during the trip. Exact terms will be specified on your invoice.

Driver Waiting Policy & Airport Pickup

Complimentary waiting time is provided for airport pickups based on standard industry practices. Extended delays not caused by flight schedules may incur additional waiting charges.

Itinerary Changes & Responsibilities

While we strive to adhere to the planned itinerary, changes may occur due to unforeseen circumstances. Travellers are expected to cooperate with drivers and guides, maintain punctuality, and follow local guidelines.

Force Majeure & Liability Limitations

MungPhai Trips & Tours shall not be held liable for delays, cancellations, or damages arising from acts of God, extreme weather, strikes, political unrest, or other events beyond our reasonable control. Vehicle availability is subject to change based on road and weather conditions in mountainous terrains.

Dispute Resolution

Any disputes arising from these terms or our services shall be subject to amicable negotiation. Unresolved disputes shall be governed by the laws of India and subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Assam.

3. Refund & Cancellation Policy

Cancellation Timelines

  • Cancellation before travel: Cancellations made well in advance may be eligible for a partial or full refund (minus administrative fees) depending on the package.
  • Cancellation after booking confirmation: Standard cancellation charges will apply as outlined in your specific booking agreement.
  • No-show policy: Failure to appear for the scheduled trip without prior notice will result in a 100% cancellation fee.

Supplier-Specific Rules

Please note that certain components of your trip are governed by third-party policies:

  • Hotel cancellation policy: Dependent strictly on individual hotel guidelines.
  • Cab booking cancellation policy: Specific notice periods apply for standalone cab rentals.
  • Tour package cancellation policy: Mixed policies apply depending on the inclusion of hotels, permits, and third-party activities.

Refund Timelines

Approved refunds will be processed back to the original method of payment within 7-14 business days, depending on bank processing times.

Need Help Regarding Your Privacy?

Reach out to our team for any clarifications on our legal terms or privacy measures.
