About MungPhai Trips & Tours

Guwahati's premier travel agency and cab rental company dedicated to bespoke luxury travel across Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh & Nagaland.

OUR HERITAGE & MISSION

Guwahati's Expedition Pioneers

MungPhai Trips & Tours was founded with a simple vision to make travel across Northeast India safe, comfortable, and unforgettable. What started as a passion for exploring the beauty of Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh, and Nagaland has grown into a trusted travel company dedicated to creating meaningful journeys.

The name "MungPhai" reflects our connection to the rich heritage and natural beauty of the region. We believe that every trip is more than just reaching a destination it's about discovering new places, creating lasting memories, and experiencing local culture.

From airport transfers and weekend getaways to customized family vacations and adventure tours, we focus on reliable service, transparent pricing, and personalized experiences. Every journey is planned with care so that you can travel with confidence and peace of mind.

At MungPhai Trips & Tours, we're not just taking you from one place to another we're helping you discover the heart of Northeast India, one journey at a time.
